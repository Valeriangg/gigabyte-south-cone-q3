01 H2'26 South Cone Marketing Strategy

Securing our gaming monitor leadership across the region — every front applies to all 4 markets and runs in a coordinated way.

01

Dominate Top Tier

Grow South Cone gaming monitor share into category leadership.

  • Positioning training with Blu across Meta & Google Ads

02

Brand Vision

Build brand recognition where it matters most — mass media.
  • Elevate GIGABYTE / AORUS recognition in mass media
  • Extend share of voice beyond the core gaming audience
Applies to all 4 markets
03

Create Local Hero

Make OLED gaming monitors the category's flagship story.
  • Position OLED monitors as the Local Hero of the segment
